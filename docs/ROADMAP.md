# Research and engineering roadmap

## Phase 0 — governance and analytical foundation

Deliverables:

- contributor and invention records;
- counsel-reviewed confidentiality and assignment plan;
- IRB/human-subject and biosafety determinations;
- process hazard and waste review;
- locked vocabulary, evidence classes, and data schema;
- gold-assay validation and closed-balance test using nonbiological controls.

Exit gate: G0 passed and all oversight approvals documented.

## Phase 1 — source-platform feasibility

Deliverables:

- qualified urine-derived cell-bank concept;
- lot identity, stability, and traceability requirements;
- reference cell platform for method development;
- reproducibility estimates across lots;
- secretome release and viable-cell exclusion assays.

Exit gate: G1 passed. If not, preserve the cell-free architecture while changing the production
chassis or stopping the urine-derived claim.

## Phase 2 — capture discovery

Deliverables:

- preregistered conditioned-versus-matched-medium study;
- positive and negative control performance;
- stage-resolved gold mass balance;
- candidate fraction/activity map;
- explicit stop/go decision.

Exit gate: G2 passed with replicated evidence.

## Phase 3 — mechanism and selectivity

Deliverables:

- active-component attribution;
- capacity and affinity measurement;
- mixed-ion selectivity profile;
- pH, redox, ionic-strength, and organic-matrix robustness map;
- orthogonal confirmation of gold chemical form.

Exit gate: G3–G5 passed. If only binding is demonstrated, retain a capture-only claim.

## Phase 4 — reusable cell-free module

Deliverables:

- soluble versus immobilized format comparison;
- breakthrough and loading model;
- capacity-retention curve by cycle;
- candidate regeneration envelope;
- leaching, carryover, and storage-stability data;
- waste characterization.

Exit gate: G6 passed or a documented single-use decision with updated economics.

## Phase 5 — integrated closed pilot

Deliverables:

- locked P&ID and interlock specification;
- qualified secretome and feed lot release;
- metered material, energy, labor, and waste flows;
- independently assayed concentrate;
- uncertainty-propagated mass balance;
- failed-run and deviation report;
- independently reviewed techno-economic and lifecycle screening.

Exit gate: G7 passed. Status may then move to `pilot-validated` for the exact bounded configuration.

## Phase 6 — commercialization decision

Decision inputs:

- comparison with incumbent capture and refining processes;
- feed-source agreements and concentration variability;
- cell-manufacturing cost and scale risk;
- environmental and occupational controls;
- IP position and freedom to operate;
- product assay, custody, and refining terms;
- waste and discharge permits;
- credible sensitivity and downside cases.

No phase is advanced because of sunk cost, narrative appeal, or a favorable synthetic model.
