# U.S. regulatory and governance map

This is an issue-spotting map, not legal advice. Applicability changes with jurisdiction, funding,
feed chemistry, engineering method, scale, waste classification, claims, and product disposition.

| Domain | Trigger to assess | Responsible review |
|---|---|---|
| Human-subject protection | Prospective collection of urine from living donors for research | IRB/human-research protection office |
| Biospecimen privacy | Identifiable or coded donor material and linked metadata | Privacy, security, and IRB offices |
| Recombinant/synthetic nucleic acids | Engineered cell line or construct within applicable scope | Institutional biosafety committee |
| Occupational exposure | Human specimens, corrosives, toxic metals, aerosols, nanoparticles | EHS/industrial hygiene |
| Hazard communication | Hazardous chemicals in workplace use | EHS and laboratory management |
| Hazardous waste | Spent leachate, mixed-metal residuals, contaminated solids | Environmental compliance office |
| Wastewater/discharge | Any proposed sewer, surface-water, or reuse pathway | Facility and permitting authority |
| Transport | Shipment of human material, engineered cells, chemicals, or waste | Shipping/compliance office |
| Product and trade | Sale, assay, custody, or refining of recovered precious metal | Commercial counsel and refiner |
| Environmental claims | “Green,” lower-impact, or waste-reduction marketing | Lifecycle-analysis and advertising review |
| Intellectual property | Public disclosure, inventorship, donor agreements, background IP | Qualified patent counsel |

## Decision sequence

1. Define the exact material, engineering method, feed, scale, facility, and output.
2. Obtain a written institutional determination for human-subject and biosafety oversight.
3. Complete process hazard, occupational exposure, and waste determinations.
4. Confirm local and state requirements in addition to federal baselines.
5. Approve transport, chain-of-custody, and external-refining agreements.
6. Reassess before changing scale, feed source, construct, facility, or commercial claim.
