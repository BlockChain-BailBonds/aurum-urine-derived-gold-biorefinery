# Validation plan

## Purpose

The validation program is designed to disprove weak versions of the AURUM hypothesis early. It
does not prescribe unsupervised cell culture, genetic modification, metal leaching, or nanoparticle
handling. All biological and chemical work requires appropriately reviewed institutional
facilities, trained personnel, and approved protocols.

## Preregistration package

Before generating comparative data, freeze:

- the primary endpoint and unit;
- sample-size rationale and replicate hierarchy;
- exclusion criteria;
- blank and control acceptance limits;
- calibration model and reportable range;
- mass-balance closure interval;
- candidate-selection rule;
- statistical model and uncertainty method;
- stop conditions and deviation process.

Exploration is permitted but must be labeled exploratory and must not retroactively change a
confirmatory endpoint.

## Gate G0 — measurement fitness

**Question:** Can the analytical system quantify gold in every relevant matrix without a false
signal from contamination, carryover, or matrix suppression?

Minimum evidence:

- reagent, vessel, field, process, and carryover blanks;
- matrix-matched calibration or a justified equivalent;
- certified reference material where available;
- spike recovery across the intended range;
- dilution integrity and instrument drift monitoring;
- independent assay of selected samples by an orthogonal method;
- defined limits of blank, detection, and quantification.

**Failure:** No biological conclusion is allowed until the measurement failure is corrected and
the affected sequence repeated.

## Gate G1 — cell-source reproducibility

**Question:** Can coded urine-derived cell lots be established reproducibly under approved
conditions?

Minimum evidence:

- documented consent and specimen provenance;
- predefined cell identity and purity panel;
- viability, expansion, and lot-release results;
- contamination controls;
- donor-to-donor and passage-associated variability estimates;
- a locked cell-bank and traceability scheme.

**Failure:** If identity, stability, or reproducibility is inadequate, switch to a qualified
reference cell system for mechanism research or terminate the donor-derived platform claim.

## Gate G2 — secretome-attributed capture

**Question:** Does released, cell-free conditioned material capture more gold than the matched
matrix without cells?

Required comparison set:

- qualified conditioned material;
- matched unconditioned medium;
- process blank;
- inactivated conditioned material;
- a positive capture control with independently established behavior;
- no-gold control for carryover.

Primary response is closed-balance gold removed from the feed and recovered in the capture phase,
not disappearance from a single liquid measurement.

**Failure:** A candidate that does not exceed the preregistered control criterion is stopped. A
post-hoc subgroup does not rescue it.

## Gate G3 — active-component attribution

**Question:** Can activity be associated with a reproducible molecular fraction or construct?

Minimum evidence:

- activity follows fractionation;
- depletion of the candidate reduces activity;
- add-back or independently produced candidate restores activity;
- identity and quantity are measured orthogonally;
- the matched matrix remains negative.

**Failure:** Report “conditioned-medium-associated capture” only. Do not claim a mechanism.

## Gate G4 — selectivity and matrix robustness

**Question:** Is gold capture retained in a chemically representative mixed-ion feed?

Report:

- gold distribution coefficient or equivalent capacity metric;
- selectivity relative to each major competing element;
- effects of pH, ionic strength, redox state, and dissolved organic matter;
- total element mass balance, not only gold;
- product contamination and downstream-refining consequences.

**Failure:** A candidate that functions only in a simplified single-ion solution remains a probe,
not a process candidate.

## Gate G5 — chemical-form confirmation

**Question:** Is recovered material elemental Au(0), an ionic complex, or a mixture?

Use at least two orthogonal analytical principles appropriate to oxidation state, crystal
structure, composition, or particle morphology. Microscopy color or appearance alone is not
sufficient.

**Failure:** Describe the result as binding or concentration. Do not claim mineralization.

## Gate G6 — reuse and stability

**Question:** Does the capture phase retain useful activity through a predetermined use and
storage cycle?

Report capacity and selectivity by cycle, regeneration losses, leaching of the active component,
carryover, shelf stability, and failure mode. Feed batches must be independently assayed.

**Failure:** Reclassify as single-use and update the waste and economic models.

## Gate G7 — integrated pilot accounting

**Question:** Does the separated two-loop architecture operate with complete material, energy,
waste, labor, and quality records?

Minimum evidence:

- independently assayed feed and product;
- stage-resolved gold residuals and uncertainty;
- energy metering by unit operation;
- consumable and labor records;
- hazardous-waste characterization;
- deviation and failed-run inclusion;
- blinded or independent data review.

**Failure:** The status remains bench-validated. Scale, revenue, or environmental claims are not
promoted.

## Statistical reporting

- Preserve biological donor, cell-lot, secretome-lot, and technical replicate levels.
- Do not treat repeated measurements of one preparation as independent biological replicates.
- Report estimates and intervals, not only thresholded p-values.
- Include all preregistered runs and explain every exclusion.
- Report negative and null outcomes.
- Propagate analytical uncertainty through the gold mass balance.
- Make raw instrument exports, transformation code, and an immutable analysis manifest available
  to authorized reviewers.
