## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-16

- Established the AURUM two-loop, cell-free secretome architecture.
- Defined the atomic boundary: recovery of pre-existing gold, not biological transmutation.
- Added scientific-basis, evidence-ledger, validation, safety, regulatory, IP, and roadmap docs.
- Added strict scenario schema and synthetic reference fixture.
- Implemented exact-decimal, mass-conserving batch and campaign model.
- Added secretome capacity, reuse decay, sensitivity analysis, CLI, tests, coverage, linting, and CI.
